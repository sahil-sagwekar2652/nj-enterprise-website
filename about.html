OUR HERITAGE & MISSION

Built for Demanding Indian & Global Conditions

At NJ Enterprises, we specialize in the end-to-end tooling, precision injection moulding, and custom fabrication of high-grade electronic and electrical enclosure systems. Under the dedicated leadership of Shrikant Naik, we have earned the trust of industrial automation engineers, OEM equipment builders, and electrical contractors across India.

From extreme monsoon rains and high-temperature solar rooftop arrays to rigorous laboratory test benches and factory floor machinery, our enclosures are crafted using premium flame-retardant ABS and UV-stabilized Polycarbonate (PC) to guarantee structural integrity and long-term durability.
