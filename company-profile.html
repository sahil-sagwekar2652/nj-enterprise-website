ABOUT US

Plastoguard Industries

Plastoguard Industries is a solutions-oriented business serving customers with a diverse range of industrial enclosures, instrument cases, cabinets and HDPE piping products. Our portfolio brings together equipment-protection solutions in ABS plastic and MS (mild steel), alongside HDPE pipes, fittings and fusion-joining products.

We aim to support OEMs, electrical and instrumentation companies, contractors, fabricators, infrastructure projects and industrial users with suitable products, responsive communication and practical commercial solutions. Product specifications can be discussed according to application, drawings and project requirements.

REQUIREMENTS

Quality & Specification

We recognize that product suitability and specification clarity are essential in both equipment enclosures and piping systems. We work to confirm relevant requirements before supply, including material, dimensions, product type, pressure class or rating where applicable, connection type and customer-specified details.

For enclosure enquiries, share a drawing, sample, dimensions and cut-out or mounting details. For HDPE piping, specify pipe size, PE grade, SDR/PN rating, applicable standard, fitting type, quantity and project location where relevant. Final availability and specifications should be confirmed at quotation stage.

Enquiry Note: Share drawings, specifications or a bill of quantities for enquiries. Final availability and specifications should be confirmed at quotation stage.
