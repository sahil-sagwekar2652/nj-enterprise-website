## Audience & Purpose
Infrastructure pipeline contractors, municipal water supply engineers, gas distribution EPCs, and industrial plant managers sourcing certified PE100/PE80 electrofusion, butt fusion, flanges, and fabricated fittings.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
A high-capacity industrial pipeline engineering matrix that rejects consumer e-commerce cards and generic plastic catalog grids. The interface presents technical pipeline fittings with parametric clarity—structured by fusion chemistry, SDR pressure ratings, standards compliance (IS 8360, ISO 4427, DIN 2501, ANSI B16.5), and direct engineering quote workflows.

### OWN-WORLD
Inherits the "Industrial Precision Bench" system: Technical Surface Ice background (`#f8f9ff`), Machinery Deep Navy (`#020617`) structural anchors, Drafting CAD Slate (`#334155`) 1px hairline border grids, and Signal Safety Orange (`#F97316`) for active pressure class flags and RFQ CTAs. JetBrains Mono typography enforces exact millimetre dimensions and pressure classes.

### STORY
The visitor immediately recognizes PlastoGuard as a certified heavy-duty HDPE piping and fittings manufacturer, navigates the 4 core product categories via high-density engineering matrices, filters by required SDR/PN ratings and diameters, and submits multi-item RFQs directly.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
A blueprint-grounded hero banner featuring live pressure rating badges (PN6, PN10, PN16, SDR11, SDR17), a technical schematic overview of electrofusion and butt fusion joints, quick-jump links to the four categories, and a prominent "Download Engineering Matrix / Request Pipe Fitting Quote" primary CTA.
