CORPORATE & MANUFACTURING PROFILE

PlastoGuard Corporate Profile

India's specialized manufacturer of high-precision electronic plastic enclosures, instrument housings, and custom CNC fabricated packaging systems for industrial and OEM applications.

OVERVIEW & IDENTITY

Engineered Protection for Mission-Critical Electronics

PlastoGuard is an Indian manufacturing enterprise dedicated to the design, precision tooling, injection moulding, and machining of high-grade electronic enclosures. Serving electronics manufacturers, automation integrators, solar developers, and testing laboratories across India, we bridge the gap between world-class protective engineering and cost-effective local manufacturing.

Our portfolio spans across 9 distinct series including IP65/IP67 weatherproof junction boxes, sloped desktop instrument cases, DIN rail enclosures, handheld diagnostic cases, and standard Indian Series enclosures and accessories.

INFRASTRUCTURE & PROCESSES

End-to-End In-House Manufacturing Capabilities

From mould design and polymer compounding to high-precision CNC routing and screen printing, our Mumbai manufacturing facility is optimized for rapid turnaround and stringent dimensional consistency.

precision_manufacturing

High-Precision Moulding

High-pressure injection moulding machines with multi-cavity hardened steel dies ensuring uniform wall thickness, zero warping, and exact snap-fit tolerances.

hardware

CNC Milling & Cutouts

Dedicated multi-axis CNC routing for display apertures, cable gland holes, switch cutouts, terminal knockouts, and custom beveling with ±0.1mm accuracy.

shield

Sealing & Ingress Tech

Seamless polyurethane (PU) and neoprene continuous foam-in-place gaskets providing IP65 to IP67 protection against dust, high-pressure water jets, and corrosive mist.

science

Quality & Impact Lab

Thermal endurance chambers (-40°C to +80°C), IK08 impact testing, UL94 flammability verification, and optical coordinate measuring machines (CMM).
