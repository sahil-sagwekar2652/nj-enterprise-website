BENCHTOP & LABORATORY CABINET

Plastic Cabinet 15 Series

Neat and sturdy table-top cabinets featuring sloped front display panels and built-in cooling air louvers. Designed for clear viewing of meters, displays, and controls on workbenches and desks.

Common Applications: Laboratory test instruments, digital multimeters, battery chargers, power supply units, and audio equipment.
