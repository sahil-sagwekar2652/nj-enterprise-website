PlastoGuard Engineering Benchmarks
1. Superior Abrasion Resistance

Tests prove HDPE has a 3:1 performance ratio over X-52 grade steel for slurries and iron ore conveyance.

2. Low Friction (C = 150)

Mirror-smooth bore with William-Hazen C=150 allows smaller diameter selection and huge lifetime pumping power savings.

3. Extreme Flexibility (20-40D)

Can be curved around obstacles and ground contours, drastically reducing elbows and trench preparation costs.

4. 100% Monolithic Welds

Molecular heat fusion creates homogenous joints stronger than the parent pipe, eliminating joint leaks forever.

5. Chemical & Biological Inertness

Unaffected by acidic soils, corrosive effluents, electrolytic currents, micro-organisms, or scaling encrustations.

6. Lightweight Density (0.95 g/cm³)

Weighs 1/8th of concrete and 1/6th of steel; enables rapid field deployment, easier handling, and lower freight charges.

High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) Pipes Portfolio

Manufactured from virgin PE100 and PE80 compound with specialized co-extruded color identification stripes for dedicated application sectors.

1. HDPE Potable Water Transmission Pipes BLUE STRIPE

Solid black with co-extruded longitudinal Blue stripes. Conforms to IS 4984:2016 and ISO 4427-2 for urban drinking water supply mains, lift irrigation pumping lines, and rural drinking schemes. Odorless, non-toxic, and biological inertness certified.

2. HDPE City Gas Distribution (CGD) Pipes YELLOW STRIPE

Engineered with high Rapid Crack Propagation (RCP) resistance and ESCR > 10,000 hrs. Designed for pressurized methane, natural gas, and LPG networks under ISO 4437 and EN 1555 standards. Operating pressure up to PN 16 (SDR 11).

3. Industrial Effluent, Slurry & Mining Pipes SOLID BLACK

Heavy-wall PE100 / PE4710 resin with outstanding abrasion resistance. Conveys coal/iron ore slurries, cement clinker, fly ash, paper pulp, and highly aggressive chemical process effluents without wall wear or chemical breakdown.

4. Agricultural Irrigation & Submersible Coils CONTINUOUS COILS

Supplied in seamless 50m, 100m, 200m, and 500m coils in outer diameters from 20mm to 110mm. Eliminates intermittent joints, preventing leak paths in micro-drip networks, agricultural sprinkler fields, and borehole submersible drop pipes.

5. Sewerage, Drainage & Underwater Submarine Outfall Pipelines BROWN STRIPE / LARGE BORE UP TO 1600 MM

Smooth hydraulic boundary prevents grease and sewage silt deposition. Non-reactive to hydrogen sulfide gas (H₂S) eliminating crown corrosion common in concrete sewers. Large-bore diameters up to 1600mm for submarine outfalls, desalination intakes, and dredging.

Straight Pipe Lengths: Standard 6-meter and 12-meter straight sticks for sizes 63mm to 1600mm. Custom lengths on request.
Continuous Coiled Pipe: Compact transport coils for sizes 20mm to 110mm, reducing trench joints by up to 90%.
