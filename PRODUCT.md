## Platform

web

## Users
- **Primary Users**: B2B electronics and electrical Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), panel builders, instrumentation fabricators, and automation engineers across India.
- **User Situation & Job**: Sourcing robust, precision-fitting, and certified standard or custom-machined enclosures (ABS, Polycarbonate, MS mild steel) and industrial piping/tools for electronic instruments, DIN-rail automation modules, and electrical junction boxes. They need accurate mechanical dimensions, cutouts/silk-screening flexibility, reliable IP ratings (IP65/IP67), and rapid Request for Quote (RFQ) turnaround.

## Product Purpose
Plastoguard Industries (formerly NJ Enterprises) delivers high-grade industrial enclosure solutions and industrial infrastructure products (HDPE pipe systems, desoldering tools) engineered for harsh industrial operating environments. The website exists to showcase the complete enclosure product catalog across specialized series, provide downloadable datasheets, and streamline the B2B RFQ inquiry process. Success means qualified B2B leads, fast quote requests with enclosure customization specifications, and established trust with Indian OEMs and industrial buyers.

## Positioning
Direct OEM tooling and manufacturing flexibility: Plastoguard bridges the gap between mass-market low-quality plastic boxes and expensive imported industrial enclosures. We offer factory-direct manufacturing in India with in-house customization (CNC milling, customized panel cutouts, silk-screen branding) and rapid sample delivery, backed by verified IP65/IP67 ingress protection.

## Operating Context
- **B2B Procurement & Engineering**: Evaluated by electrical design engineers, CAD drafters, and procurement managers comparing physical enclosure dimensions, PCB mounting bosses, flame retardancy, and ingress protection against project schematics.
- **Workflow**: Catalog browsing -> technical spec comparison across series (11-series, 15-series, 18-series, 21-series, 23-series, Indian series) -> RFQ submission with custom requirements -> direct technical consultation and dispatch.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Product Range**: ABS & Polycarbonate enclosures (desktop, handheld, DIN-rail, wall-mount, junction box), MS mild steel cabinets, HDPE pipes/fittings, and desoldering tools.
- **Customization**: Custom cutouts for connectors, displays, switches, vents; silk-screen printing of client logos and legends.
- **Web Platform Capabilities**: Interactive responsive catalog with series filtering, dynamic search, multi-image product showcases, direct RFQ modal/page with email notification routing (via Resend API and Supabase/form integration).
- **Constraints**: Static multi-page web architecture (HTML5 + Tailwind CSS + Vanilla JS, built via Vite).

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone & Voice**: Authoritative, technically precise, reliable, and manufacturer-direct. Focused on durability, engineering specs, and industrial compliance.
- **Visual Identity Preservation**: Professional industrial aesthetic; high-contrast technical clarity; preserve existing product codes and category organization.

## Evidence on Hand
- Full product catalog with real model codes (e.g. NJ 10106, NJ 11-88, NJ 18-34, NJ 19-12) and dimensional specifications.
- Verified physical attributes: Polycarbonate & ABS materials, IP65 and IP67 waterproof certification ratings.

## Product Principles
1. **Spec First**: Prioritize exact dimensions, material composition, IP ratings, and mounting boss layouts over vague marketing adjectives.
2. **Frictionless RFQ**: Keep quote requests immediate and accessible from every product and series view.
3. **Manufacture-Direct Transparency**: Clearly communicate custom tooling capabilities (cutouts, silk-screening, custom colors) alongside standard off-the-shelf models.
4. **Performance & Lightweight Web Delivery**: Fast-loading, highly legible, accessible pages that render effortlessly on mobile and desktop field environments.
